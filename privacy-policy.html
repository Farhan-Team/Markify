Privacy Policy

How Markify X handles information when you visit our website or use our digital tools.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Markify X (“Markify X,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with our website, services, and digital tools, including the LinkedIn Agent application where available. By using our services, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use.

Important: The LinkedIn Agent may provide features for drafting content, managing a personal workflow, tracking job applications, and reviewing performance. LinkedIn account data is accessed only if you choose to connect your account and authorize the permissions requested for an enabled feature.

1. Information we may collect

  • Contact and inquiry information: information you provide when contacting us, requesting a consultation, or communicating about our services.
  • Website and device information: technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate usage data, and diagnostic logs that may be generated when you use our website or tools.
  • Information you enter into our tools: drafts, notes, job opportunities, application stages, prospect records, and performance figures you choose to save.
  • LinkedIn data, if you authorize a connection: information made available through the LinkedIn API and the specific permissions you approve, which may include profile details or content and engagement data needed to provide a requested feature. The exact data depends on the permissions LinkedIn grants and the features you use.

2. How we use information

We may use information to provide and operate our website and tools; generate content drafts and recommendations you request; organize records you enter; display performance information; respond to inquiries; maintain security; troubleshoot errors; and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not use LinkedIn data for purposes unrelated to the feature you requested or the permissions you authorized.

3. LinkedIn and third-party services

If you connect LinkedIn, your use of LinkedIn remains subject to LinkedIn’s own terms and privacy policy. Our application can access only the data and actions permitted by LinkedIn and authorized by you. API access is subject to LinkedIn’s approval, restrictions, and platform policies. We do not claim to provide features that LinkedIn has not approved or made available.

We may rely on service providers that host or support our website and tools. Such providers may process information on our behalf to deliver those services and are expected to protect it appropriately. Third-party websites and services operate under their own policies.

4. Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Some current workflow records may be stored in your browser on the device you use. Browser-local records generally do not automatically synchronize across devices and may be lost if you clear browser data or remove site storage. Do not enter passwords, authentication codes, or other highly sensitive information into ordinary notes or drafts.

If a feature uses server-side storage or a LinkedIn connection, its handling of information will depend on the feature’s implementation and the permissions granted. Authentication tokens, where used, should be handled as confidential credentials and not shared with other people.

5. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that help operate our services, when you direct us to do so, when necessary to protect rights or security, or when disclosure is required by law. We do not share LinkedIn data with third parties except as needed to provide the requested service, as authorized by you, or as legally required.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. You can delete browser-local records through the relevant tool controls where available or by clearing the site’s browser storage. To request deletion of information held by us, or to disconnect a LinkedIn integration where that feature is available, contact us through the Markify X contact section. Some records may need to be retained where legally required or for legitimate security purposes.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can choose not to provide optional information and should disconnect third-party integrations you no longer wish to use. We will review privacy requests and respond as required by applicable law.

8. Children’s privacy

Our website and professional tools are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. International processing

Depending on the hosting and service providers used, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied to such transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, technology, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when this page was most recently revised. Continued use after an update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Markify X through the contact section on our website. Please do not include passwords, access tokens, or other confidential credentials in your message.
