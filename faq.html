MARKIFY X / FAQ

Questions before you start?

Straightforward answers about how Markify X works, what we offer, and what you can expect. If your situation is specific, bring the problem to a consultation rather than trying to fit it into a standard package.

18 practical answers
01

Working with Markify X

What does Markify X do?

Markify X combines technology, marketing, creative services and business automation. We can help with websites, apps, SaaS and custom software, SEO and AI search, digital marketing, social media, branding, video, eCommerce, email marketing and automation.

Do you work with startups and established businesses?

Yes. We work with businesses at different stages and can scope a project around a specific growth problem, a new build, an existing system or an ongoing marketing requirement.

Can Markify X handle both development and marketing?

Yes. Development and marketing can be handled as connected workstreams when a business needs both. This can reduce gaps between the website, tracking, acquisition and conversion experience.

How do you decide what a business actually needs?

We start with the business goal and the current bottleneck rather than assuming a particular service is required. We review the situation, identify practical priorities and then recommend the work that supports those priorities.

Do you offer one-time projects or ongoing support?

Both are possible. A project can be scoped as a defined engagement, while ongoing support can cover continued development, marketing, optimization, reporting or automation.

What happens after I request a consultation?

We review the information you provide, discuss your goals and current situation, identify the main opportunities or constraints, and determine whether there is a practical project to scope.

02

SEO, search & growth

Do you provide SEO and AI search optimization?

Yes. Our search work covers traditional SEO, technical SEO, local SEO, content strategy, answer engine optimization and optimization for AI-powered search experiences.

How long does SEO take to show results?

There is no universal timeline. Results depend on the market, competition, website condition, authority, content quality and the work being implemented. We focus on measurable improvements and review progress over time rather than promising a fixed ranking date.

Do you provide local SEO for businesses with physical locations?

Yes. Local SEO can include location targeting, Google Business Profile support, local landing pages, citations, reputation-related work, technical improvements and content designed around local search intent.

03

Web, software & eCommerce

Can you work on an existing website instead of rebuilding it?

Yes. Where the existing platform is suitable, we can improve it rather than replacing it. The approach depends on the site's technology, condition, performance and business requirements.

Do you build Shopify and WooCommerce stores?

Yes. We work with Shopify and WooCommerce for store development, customization, technical improvements, SEO, conversion work and ongoing growth support.

Can you build custom SaaS or internal business software?

Yes. We can help plan and build SaaS products, dashboards, CRM and ERP-style systems, internal tools, APIs and integrations based on the required workflow and technical scope.

Can Markify X connect marketing data and business systems?

Yes. Where the platforms support it, we can connect analytics, marketing tools, websites, CRM systems, forms, APIs and other business systems to improve reporting and workflow automation.

04

Marketing & creative

Do you offer social media management?

Yes. Social media work can include strategy, content planning, creative production, publishing, community management and campaign support depending on the engagement.

Do you run paid advertising campaigns?

Yes. Paid media support can include campaign planning, audience and offer strategy, landing pages, tracking, creative coordination and ongoing performance optimization.

Can you manage email marketing and automation?

Yes. We can support newsletters, campaigns, segmentation, automated journeys, lead nurturing, customer retention and integrations with business systems.

Do you provide branding, graphic design and video editing?

Yes. Creative services include brand identity, graphic design, marketing assets, social creatives, presentations, short-form video, advertisements and other business content.

05

Scope, pricing & process

How is a project scoped and priced?

Pricing depends on the work required, technical complexity, number of deliverables, integrations, timeline and level of ongoing support. We define scope before confirming the commercial arrangement.

Don't see your question?

Every business has different constraints. Tell us what you are trying to build, fix, improve or grow, and we can discuss the relevant path.

Have a specific business problem?

Start with the problem, not the service. We can discuss the current situation, what is blocking progress and which workstreams may actually be useful.
